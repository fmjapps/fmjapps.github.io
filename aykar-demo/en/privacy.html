  • To keep the site secure.

4. Legal grounds

We process your data on the grounds set out in Article 5(2) of the KVKK: performance of the contract relating to the product you bought (c), compliance with our legal obligations (ç), establishing, exercising or defending a right (e), and our legitimate interests, provided they do not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (f).

5. How we collect it

Data is collected electronically, partly by automated means: through messages you prepare with the form on our site and send yourself from your own WhatsApp or email app, through phone calls, and through the site's technical infrastructure. The form does not send or store anything unless you send the message yourself.

Only as needed for the purposes above, your data may be shared with authorised public bodies on request, our legal advisers and service providers we work with (e.g. website hosting, email).

If you choose to write to us on WhatsApp, your data is also subject to that service's terms. You can also reach us by phone. Transfers abroad are made in line with Article 9 of the KVKK.

7. How long we keep it

Data about your report is kept for [2 years] after the report is closed, or longer if required by law. It is then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request deletion or destruction under the conditions of Article 7; request that these actions be notified to third parties; object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

9. How to apply

To exercise your rights, send a written request to the address above, or email info@marmarayotomat.com.tr from the email address you used with us. We will respond free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
