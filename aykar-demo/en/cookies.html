Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on marmarayotomat.com.

Our own cookies

Our site does not use cookies for advertising, marketing or visitor tracking, and it has no analytics tools.

On the Turkish page, if your browser language is different, we suggest the version of the site in your language. If you close that suggestion, a small flag is written to your browser's session storage (sessionStorage) so it is not shown again in the same session. This information is never sent to us and is deleted when you close the tab.

Strictly necessary technical cookies

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may set short-lived technical cookies when needed to protect the site from attacks and malicious traffic. These are required for the site to work securely and are not used to identify you.

Third-party content

  • Google Maps: The machine map is not loaded until you press "Show map" or choose a station. Once it loads, Google may use cookies under its own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp links take you to wa.me, where WhatsApp's (Meta) own policies apply.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect parts of the site.

Contact

For questions, write to info@marmarayotomat.com.tr. For details on your personal data, see our Privacy Notice.
