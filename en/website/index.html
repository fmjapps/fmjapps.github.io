    Domain and hosting

    You get the domain, we take care of the rest.

    Choosing a hosting company, setting up a server, renewing security certificates, keeping the site online… You don’t have to deal with any of it.

    What you do

    • Register your domain (e.g. yourbusiness.com) in your own name.
    • Pay the yearly domain fee directly to the domain registrar.
    • The domain always stays yours; you’re never tied to anyone.

    What we do

    • Set up the hosting for your site, with no separate hosting fee for you.
    • Connect your domain to the site and set up HTTPS.
    • Launch the site and keep it running.
    No payment system

    The site has no shopping cart or online payments. It presents your business; customers reach you by phone, WhatsApp or the contact form.

    FAQ

    Questions you might have

    Where do I get a domain?

    From any domain registrar, in your own name. If you’re not sure which one to choose, we’ll look at it together.

    Will I pay a separate hosting fee?

    No. We set up and manage the hosting for your site; the only thing you need to get is the domain.

    Can I sell online through my site?

    No, we don’t set up payment systems. Your site presents your business; customers reach you by phone, WhatsApp or the contact form.

    Who prepares the text and photos?

    You send us the information and photos; we turn the text into something that reads well on the site and send it for your approval.

    Can things be changed later?

    Yes. Tell us what you’d like changed; we’ll agree on the scope together and update your site.

    Do you offer SEO?

    We set up SEO basics on every site: page titles and descriptions, a clean heading structure, structured data, a sitemap, fast loading and Search Console registration. We don’t guarantee a specific position on Google; rankings build over time, depending on your content and your competitors.

    How much does it cost?

    Write to us for pricing; we’ll send you a quote that fits what you need.
