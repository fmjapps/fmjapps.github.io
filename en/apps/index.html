    Lost & Found Office

    Daily deduction game

    You're behind the counter of the Central Station lost & found, and on top of it sits Pamuk, the office cat. Six visitors come in every day: some really are looking for their suitcase, some are impostors eyeing an item on the notice board. Check the report, ask three questions, make your call.

    • A new shift every day
    • Same visitors for everyone worldwide

    The Collector: Real or Fake

    Antique & pawn shop game

    Your grandfather's antique shop is yours now. Customers bring pocket watches, coins and paintings; turn them over, sweep the loupe, open the catalogue. Spot the fakes, haggle, and gather works by eighteen great masters in a single showcase.

    • Loupe, UV lamp, pigment analysis
    • Haggle with sellers
    • An 18-master collection
    • 8–10 minute shop days

    Games03 / 03

    Prizma

    Light and mirror puzzles

    Place the mirrors and guide the beam to its targets. The rules look simple, then come prism blocks that split the light, portals that send it out somewhere else and colours that mix like real light. On Hard and Master the beam stays hidden: set your mirrors first, then switch on the light.

    • 60 levels, 4 difficulties
    • A daily puzzle, same for everyone
    • Play Games leaderboard and achievements
    • Colour-blind mode

    Paydos

    Screen lock for kids

    Put a gentle limit on your child’s phone time. Set the time, tick the apps they can open and hand the phone over. When time is up, or a blocked app is opened, the screen locks and only your PIN unlocks it. Your child can always see how much time is left.

    • Pick the time and the apps
    • PIN-protected full-screen lock
    • No data leaves the phone
    • Completely free, 9 languages

    How we build

    No accounts

    None of our apps ask you to sign up or hand over an email. Open it and go.

    Your data on your phone

    Our apps have no servers of their own. Your progress, texts and settings are stored on your phone; in Prizma you can also use Google Play Games cloud saves if you like.

    Fair play

    You win by paying attention, not by guessing. In Lost & Found Office and Prizma, each day’s content is the same for everyone in the world.

    Nine languages

    Our games and apps come in nine or ten languages, English and Turkish among them, and open in your phone’s language.
