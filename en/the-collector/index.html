The Collector: Real or Fake

Antique shop detective game

Your grandfather's old antique shop is yours now. In a drawer lies a leather-bound notebook, and on its first page is his unfinished dream: to gather one genuine work by eighteen great masters in a single showcase. But the market is full of fakes.

How to play

  1. Inspect

    Customers bring pocket watches, coins and paintings. Turn each item over: check the dial, the case back, the movement. Slide the loupe with your finger.

  2. Check the catalogue

    Which maker worked in which years? Which hallmark did they use? Which pigment didn't exist yet? Read the catalogue and you'll catch the forger.

  3. Haggle

    Greedy, desperate, or clueless sellers… Make the right offer and snap up a real treasure for pennies.

  4. Sell or keep

    Sell genuine pieces at auction or keep them in your showcase, where they earn visitor income every day.

Privacy policy

Questions and answers

Is The Collector free?

Yes, it's free to download and play. There are optional purchases: a permanent ad-free pack and single-use key packs.

Are there ads?

Only rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an extra shop key. There are no interstitials and no banners. With the ad-free pack you get the key without watching an ad.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the game itself plays offline.

Is spotting a fake down to luck?

No. Every item is generated from rules, and every fake can be caught with the tools you have.

Is there an iOS version?

Not for now. The game is available on Android only, through Google Play.
