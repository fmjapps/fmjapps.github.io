    Replies instantly

    The reply goes out the moment a message arrives. Even while your team is asleep, on vacation or busy with another customer.

    Speaks the customer’s language

    Turkish, English, German, Arabic, Russian and more. It replies in whatever language the customer writes in.

    Uses your information

    It explains your prices, services and rules using only the information you provide. It never makes up what it doesn’t know.

    Gets to know and saves customers

    During the chat it learns the customer’s name, needs and dates, then adds them to your dashboard as a lead.

    Hands over at the right moment

    When a customer complains, asks for a discount, has a special request or says “I’d like to speak to a person,” it hands the conversation to your team, along with the reason.

    No promises, no made-up answers

    It never guarantees results. On health, legal or financial matters it doesn’t stand in for an expert; it refers the customer to your team.
