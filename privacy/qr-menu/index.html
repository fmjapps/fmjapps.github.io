Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

QR Menu is a digital menu service FMJ Software provides to businesses. Menus are published at qr.fmjapps.com and each business manages its menu from its own panel. Under Article 10 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK), this page explains what data is processed.

01Data controller

Oğulcan Fidan – FMJ Software (Menemen tax office, tax no. 3870844488), İsmet İnönü Mah. 1267 Sk. Refah No: 12 İç Kapı No: 3, Menemen / İzmir, Türkiye. Contact: contact@fmjapps.com. The business that publishes a menu is responsible for its products, prices and descriptions.

02What we collect

Menu visitors: We ask for nothing. There are no cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and fonts are served from our own server. As required by Turkish Law No. 5651, our server logs each request's IP address, time, requested address and browser information.

Business panel users: Username, a one-way hash of the password (bcrypt; the password itself is not stored), a session cookie (qrs, 30 days, sign-in only), the menu content, logo and product photos the business enters, and access logs.

03Purposes and legal grounds

Data is collected electronically and automatically through the server and the business panel.

  • Publishing menus and running the panel: performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c).
  • Access logs: legal obligation (Law No. 5651 art. 5; KVKK art. 5/2-a, ç).
  • Security and abuse prevention: legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f).

04Third parties and transfers abroad

Data is stored on our server in Oracle Cloud's Frankfurt (Germany) data center, which means it is transferred abroad (KVKK art. 9). Data is never shared for advertising or marketing; it is disclosed only to legally authorized authorities on request.

05Retention

  • Access logs: 1 year, then deleted automatically.
  • Panel account and menu content: for the duration of the service; deleted within 30 days after it ends.

06Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 you can ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, learn who it was shared with, object, and claim compensation. Send requests to contact@fmjapps.com; we reply free of charge within 30 days.

07Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version always lives on this page and the “last updated” date changes accordingly.
